# Locking content

:::info[Premium feature]

Locking content is part of [Panels Premium](/premium/what-is-panels-premium.md).

:::

Locking lets you keep parts of your library private without affecting anything else. It works per item, so you're not choosing between locking your whole library and locking nothing.

## Locking a comic or folder

Long press any single comic or folder and choose to lock it. That item is locked on its own — everything around it stays exactly as it was.

Once locked:

- The comic or folder appears **blurred** in your library.
- Opening it requires your **password or Face ID**.

## Locked content is hidden from the Files app

This is the part worth knowing in advance: a locked file or folder is also **hidden from the file system**.

So if you lock something in Panels and then go looking for it in the Files app, you won't find it. Nothing has been deleted — it's still there, just not visible outside Panels. Unlock it in Panels and it reappears.

:::tip

Worth keeping in mind before [moving to a new device](moving-to-new-device.md) or copying your library somewhere else: unlock anything you need to see in the Files app first.

:::

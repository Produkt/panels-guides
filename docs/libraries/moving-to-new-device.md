# Moving to a new device

## Taking your content with you

:::warning

The Panels folder on your device is **not** included in your iPhone backup. Copy it somewhere safe before you erase your old device.

:::

As explained in [how libraries work](how-libraries-work.md), Panels keeps its default library in **Files → On my iPhone/iPad → Panels**.

Before wiping your old device, save a copy of that Panels folder somewhere — your Mac, an external drive, or AirDrop the whole folder straight to the new device.

Then, on the new device:

1. Install Panels.
2. Open the Files app and go to **On my iPhone/iPad → Panels**.
3. Drop in the content from your old device.
4. Open Panels. It will start reading and indexing everything.

:::tip

Using [iCloud Drive](icloud-drive.md) or [OPDS](/opds/opds.md)? There's nothing to copy — just set the library up again on the new device and your content is there.

:::

:::note

[Locked content](locking-content.md) is hidden from the file system, so unlock anything you want to copy before you start.

:::

## Taking your reading progress with you

Your reading sessions travel through your Panels account, not through the files.

Sign in on your **old** device (if you aren't already) and give it a moment to finish uploading. Then sign in with the same account on the new device and it will download everything.

There's no way to transfer sessions manually — the account is the only route.

→ [Reading progress and sync](/read-content/reading-sessions.md)

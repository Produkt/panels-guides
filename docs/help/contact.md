## Helping us help you

A few things make a real difference when you report a problem:

- **Your device and iOS version**, and the version of Panels.
- **What you expected to happen** and what happened instead.
- **A file that reproduces it**, if the problem only happens with certain comics.
- **[App diagnostics](sending-app-diagnostics.md)**, if Panels crashed. These contain the crash logs we can't see remotely, and the recent ones are the most useful.

## Before you write

Some things we get asked often have a quick answer:

- **Lost Premium after reinstalling, or on a new device?** You don't need to pay again — see [restoring purchases](/premium/restoring-purchases.md).
- **A family member can't get Premium?** Family Sharing runs on Apple's servers, so [our guide](/premium/family-sharing.md) covers the settings we can't check from our side.
- **Anything else?** Have a look through the [FAQs](/faqs.md).

:::note

Panels purchases are handled by Apple, so we can't issue refunds or see your payment details. For anything involving billing, [Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/) is the right place.

:::

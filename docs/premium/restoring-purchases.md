# Restoring purchases

Your Premium purchase belongs to your **Apple Account**, not to a single device. You never need to pay again to use it on another iPhone, iPad, or Mac, or after deleting and reinstalling Panels.

## How to restore

1. Make sure the device is signed in to the **same Apple Account** you used to buy Premium.
2. In Panels, open **Settings** and tap the **My Purchases** section.
3. On the purchase screen, tap **Restore purchases**.

Panels checks your purchases with Apple and unlocks Premium.

:::tip

If you accidentally try to buy Premium again with the same Apple Account, Apple won't charge you twice.

:::

## If restoring doesn't work

- **"Couldn't find any product to restore"**: the device is probably signed in to a different Apple Account for App Store purchases. Check **Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases** and make sure it's the account you bought Premium with.
- **Premium came from a family member**: see [Family Sharing](./family-sharing.md).
- **Still stuck?** Contact us at **support@panels.app** and include the App Store receipt for your purchase.

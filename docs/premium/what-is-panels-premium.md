# What is Panels Premium

Panels is free to download, and many of its features are free forever: you can import comics, organize your library, and read them without paying anything.

**Panels Premium** unlocks the more advanced features, like cloud libraries, OPDS servers, page filters, and text detection. A single purchase works on all your Apple devices signed in with the same Apple Account, and can be shared with your family through [Family Sharing](./family-sharing.md).

## Plans

There are two ways to get Premium.

### Panels+ (subscription)

**Panels+** is a monthly or yearly subscription. It unlocks **every** feature for as long as the subscription is active, including features added in future major versions. Both plans include a free trial.

### Panels 3 (one-time purchase)

**Panels 3** is a one-time purchase. It unlocks all Panels 3 features **forever**, including any added to Panels 3 later.

When a new major version (Panels 4) is released, you keep everything you have. New features introduced in Panels 4 will need a new purchase, and owners of the previous version get a discount.

We don't work to a fixed release schedule, but Panels 4 will most likely arrive sometime in **2026**. Since previous-version owners always get a discount, buying now doesn't cost you anything when it does.

:::tip Already bought Panels 2?

Your Panels 2 purchase is still valid and keeps its features. To get the features added in Panels 3, you can upgrade at a discounted price.

:::

## Getting Premium

In Panels, open **Settings** and tap the **My Purchases** section to see the available plans. You'll also see the plans when you try to use a Premium feature.

All payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. To manage or cancel a Panels+ subscription, follow [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).

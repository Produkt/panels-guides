# Page thumbnails and double page grouping

While reading, the horizontal carousel at the bottom of the screen shows your page thumbnails. Beyond jumping around the comic, **long pressing** a thumbnail is how you fix page grouping and share a page.

## Double page grouping

When reading in double page mode, Panels groups pages for you automatically. It keeps the cover as a single page, starts grouping from the second page, and leaves any landscape page on its own.

That's the right answer for most comics, but not all of them — a missing or extra page early on throws off everything after it.

### Fixing the grouping

Long press any page thumbnail and select **Group from this page on**.

That page becomes the start of a new grouping (placed on the left side for left-to-right reading), and the rest of the pages rearrange to follow.

In the example below, pages 2-3 are grouped initially. After tapping **Group from this page on**, pages 3-4 are grouped instead and page 2 is left on its own.

## Sharing a page {#sharing-a-page}

Long press a page in the thumbnail carousel and choose **share** to send that page elsewhere — messages, notes, or any app in the iOS share sheet.

:::tip

Want to pull text or a character out of a page rather than share the whole thing? [Live Text](live-text.md) lets you select text and subjects directly, including turning a subject into a sticker.

:::

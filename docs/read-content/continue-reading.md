# Continue reading

**Continue reading** lets you go from one issue straight into the next without going back to your library.

When you reach the last page of a comic, keep scrolling and Panels jumps to the next title.

## How the next title is chosen

Continue reading works **within a folder**. Panels moves to the next title in the same folder as the one you're reading — which is why keeping a series in its own collection is worth the effort.

:::note

Continue reading does not currently work while streaming over OPDS. We'll introduce it once OPDS 2.0 is released and adopted both by Panels and by OPDS servers.

You can still use it with titles you've [downloaded from your server](/opds/connecting-a-server.md#using-it-as-an-import-service), since those live in a folder on your device.

:::
